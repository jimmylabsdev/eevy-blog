Common questions

Before you start

Do I need exact numbers for every question?

No. Approximate figures are fine, and you can mark any answer "not sure" instead of guessing. Our team follows up on the gaps before your plan is finalised.

Do I need GPS or telematics data to do this?

No. It helps if you have it, but most businesses complete the assessment from memory and their regular fuel and maintenance records.

Is this just a sales pitch to get us to buy EVs?

No. If a vehicle or route genuinely isn't ready for EVs, your plan will say so plainly, under "Not yet suitable" — we're not paid more for recommending EVs where they don't fit.

How long does the online assessment take?

About 15–20 minutes for a small fleet. You can complete it in one sitting or come back and finish it later.

I've been asked to fill this out for my company — is that fine?

Yes. Most submissions come from a fleet manager, transport supervisor or accounts person rather than the business owner directly. Just fill in what you know and mark the rest "not sure."

Is our fleet and cost data kept confidential?

Yes. Your information is used only to prepare your EV Transition Plan and is not shared with any third party.
